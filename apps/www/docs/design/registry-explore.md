# Registry explore surface

The `/registry` page follows the user's Airbnb browsing reference: a centered search field below the global header with an integrated Filters control, and mod cards spanning the available page width. There is no map or filter sidebar. RSMM's existing warm dark palette, Alegreya Sans controls, parchment text, crimson actions, and blackletter wordmark remain authoritative.

This is an Operate surface: find a mod, narrow the results, then open or download it. The global header retains its normal navigation, version, source, and account controls. The chosen Centered toolbar sits directly beneath it, with the result count on the left, search in the center, and sort/view controls on the right. Both stay pinned throughout scrolling, including at the footer. The toolbar uses the measured header height as its top offset and reserves a fixed height in the page: 85px on desktop, 141px on tablet, and 129px on phones. Below 1100px, search moves to a full-width second line inside the same pinned toolbar. Long result headings truncate visually while retaining the full accessible text and title.

The search field places the magnifying-glass submit control on the left and a Filters button inside the right end. Filter options are available in the modal instead of exposed shortcut chips. An active-filter count sits on the Filters button without changing its size or the row's height. The result count, search, sort, and retained card/list preference share the pinned toolbar. The default card grid uses the full width, growing from one column on phones to a maximum of four columns on larger screens. Registry and homepage cards both render the shared `ModCard` component, including its default 16:9 image area (also used for generated covers), bordered surface, title, author, category, summary, downloads, rating, and version. Mature-content labels remain visible when those results are included. Existing generated covers fill missing-image states; download and app actions remain available on the detail page and in list view.

Filters opens a native modal dialog with categories, available tags and counts, minimum player rating, featured-only, and mature-content preferences. Changes remain local to the dialog until Show mods applies them together. Clear all resets the draft; closing by the close button, Escape, or backdrop discards it. The dialog traps focus, restores it on close, locks background scrolling, and keeps its header and actions visible while its body scrolls. On mobile it sits at the bottom of the viewport with safe-area padding.

Search is debounced by 300ms and can also be submitted. Applied state lives in the URL, preserving shared searches and browser navigation. Changing search, sorting, or filters restarts pagination. Clearing filters keeps search and sort; resetting an empty result clears the whole search. Server-side filtering, pagination, and mature-content exclusion remain in place. These changes require no new API endpoints or dependencies.
