Ability Editor

How this works.
  1. Pick an ability on the left.
  2. Type a new number in the box and press Enter. The row turns purple and the change is checked straight away. ↺ puts the original back.
  3. When the bar at the bottom says Ready to build, choose your mod at the bottom and press Save to mod: the changes go into its custom hero built on this hero, and come back when you open it again. Then run rsmm apply.
Nothing here touches your game until you apply. To see what triggers what, or change it, use the How it works tab.
