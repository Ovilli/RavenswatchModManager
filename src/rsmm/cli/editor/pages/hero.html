Hero Builder

Who

Your hero starts as a copy of a shipped hero: its body, moves and everything you do not change below.

Form

This hero changes form. Pick whether yours does.

Abilities

For each button, keep what the original does, take it away, or have it also fire a projectile from anywhere in the game (an enemy's included). Greyed-out choices say why this hero cannot do them yet.

Pets

Creatures that follow your hero and fight for it, Piper's way: an enemy from anywhere in the game, turned to your side. They come at the start of a run, or one batch per press of a button. Leaving a fight calls them back.

Numbers and wiring

Damage, cooldowns and everything the blocks above do not cover are in the Abilities tab. Save here first, then pick “a new hero built on it” there and choose this hero.
